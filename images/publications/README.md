## Processing

- PDF captures isolate the complete referenced figure without its surrounding body text.
- Video captures use the stated timestamp; the screenshots do not assert that the surrounding video is unedited.
- Images are proportionally resized and encoded as WebP. Diagram previews use contain, preserving every panel. Desktop demo thumbnails may crop the display; the image viewer shows the full frame.
- The HiF-VLA and VLA-Adapter project pages declare [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Derivative image files for those projects are shared under the same license.
- Public source URLs and captions are recorded in `_data/home_publication_media.json`.
